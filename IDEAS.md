# 🚀 Ryzz Feature Ideas & Improvements

Here are 10 ideas to make Ryzz even better, including the ones already implemented in this PR!

## ✅ Already Implemented in this PR

1.  **Message Character Limit & Counter**: Added a 300-character limit and a real-time counter to the message submission form to prevent spam and ensure messages fit well on shareable cards.
2.  **Profanity Filtering**: Integrated a profanity filter to maintain a positive and safe community environment by automatically masking inappropriate language.
3.  **"NEW!" Message Badges**: Enhanced the Inbox UI with a bouncing "NEW!" badge and highlighted borders for unread messages, making it easy for users to spot new secrets.
4.  **Improved Gradient Selection**: Added a gradient picker to the message sharing view, allowing users to choose their favorite style or cycle through all available gradients more easily.

## 💡 Future Ideas & Improvements

5.  **Multi-Message Sharing**: Allow users to select multiple messages and generate a single "collated" image or a carousel-style share for Instagram Stories.
6.  **Custom Prompt Creation**: Let users define their own custom prompts (e.g., "Ask me anything about my trip! ✈️") instead of just using the randomized defaults.
7.  **Emoji Reactions**: Allow the receiver of a message to react with an emoji (❤️, 😂, 😮, 😡) that the (anonymous) sender can see if they bookmark the link.
8.  **Scheduled Message Deletion**: Add an option for "self-destructing" messages that disappear from the inbox 24 or 48 hours after being read.
9.  **Advanced Analytics**: Provide a dashboard for users to see which social platform (Instagram, Snapchat, Twitter) is driving the most messages to their link.
10. **Theme Customization**: Expand the "Boss-level customization" by allowing users to choose custom fonts or upload their own background images for their message cards.
